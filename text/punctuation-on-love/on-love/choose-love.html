back to /on love

Do you wait around for her to utter something new?
I do.

I wonder if every new poem she writes is about you.
To my surprise, she writes about other things too—
nature, the trees, colours, feelings.
She is a servant of love but not consumed by it wholly.
Unlike my unquenched thirst
to be yours once again.

I decipher her stanzas,
looking for traces of you.
Every rhyme, every word, any clue.
My solar plexus boasts an envious flame.
Why can I taste her name?

A blazing campfire of oozing utterances—
why can I feel her warmth through her words,
as if she warmed them for me?

My eyes feast.
I want to eat her sentiments and sentences,
lick the crumbs of her allusions,
and wash it down with her pleasurable love.

Is this the non-attachment that you speak of?

Can’t suffering be pleasurable too?
So long as I cease to be lost in it.

She swirls around my mind,
writing laps around me.
Every vowel of imagery catalyzes me towards polarity.

I try to choose love every time.
