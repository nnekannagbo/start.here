i stripped my website.
she’s naked again.
just like all of us when we are born.
i removed the loud intro text
and rounded corners.
i let go of my desire and craving
to be featured on best folio.
i shrugged off the design trends—
skeuomorphism;
3D animation;
isometric design;
etc etc etc.
i chose to reveal her bare bones instead.
embodying brutalism,
as she is the true extension of me.
not my instagram.
not my twitter.
not a photo of me you’ll find on Meta.
she’s (more) me.
and all my corners.
shedding light on the both of us.
for you to consume—
at your delight
or displeasure.
